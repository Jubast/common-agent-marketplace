# Deep research handoff

A stronger-model deep-researcher is available after this investigation, only if warranted and the operator approves. Distill your findings into options; do not start stage 2 yourself.

End `report.md` with this section:

```markdown
## Deep research
Verdict: warranted | not warranted - <one-line reason>
### Option 1: <title>
- Summary: ...
- Evidence: <file:line, URLs>
- Open questions for stage 2: ...
- Confidence: low | medium | high
### Option 2: <title>
...
### Searched
- <queries, sources, files and approaches tried, and the dead ends ruled out>
```

Choose one verdict and confidence per option. Include zero or more options; `### Searched` is always required. With zero options, you may still say `Verdict: warranted` if a stronger model could succeed: explain why in the verdict reason and leave a non-empty search log so it can build on your work.
